# HSDSC Co-Branding

The masthead places the HSDSC school logo on the left, followed by the SDSC wordmark and the HPC Learning Hub name as one compact identity group. A divider separates the SDSC wordmark from the larger, bold Hub title, not the two institutional marks. The title remains visible rather than being pushed to the opposite edge or hidden at intermediate widths. The footer uses the school's official white version, not a CSS-recolored copy.

## Asset Sources

- Blue/gold: https://cdn.ucsd.edu/cms/decorator-5/img/logos/blue-gold/ucsdlogo-hsdsc-bluegold.png
- White: https://cdn.ucsd.edu/cms/decorator-5/img/logos/white/ucsdlogo-hsdsc-white.png
- HSDSC's official website uses the blue/gold file in its masthead.
- Usage guide: https://hsdsc.ucsd.edu/logo-usage-guide.html
- SDSC guide: https://www.sdsc.edu/about/brand.html

The downloaded PNG files are retained unmodified. Preserve aspect ratios, approved colors, and clear space of at least the relevant capital-letter height. Both masthead marks remain at least 150 CSS pixels wide; very narrow screens stack the pair rather than shrinking it. The masthead reserves at least 24px of separation and vertical space. On phones the Hub name and menu sit beneath the logo row. The footer has additional space around the larger school mark.

This local implementation follows the published guidance; it does not imply formal approval by the communications team.
